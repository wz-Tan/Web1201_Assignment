About SigmaStore

Our Story

Founded in 2020, SigmaStore started as a thrift shop in SS15, Subang Jaya, offering a curated selection of vintage clothing, streetwear, and everyday fashion at affordable prices. Conveniently located near public transport, the store quickly became a popular destination for teenagers and university students seeking stylish yet budget-friendly apparel. As interest in vintage and retro fashion continues to grow, SigmaStore is expanding its presence online, making it easier for customers to explore its latest collections anytime, anywhere.

Our Mission

Our mission is to extend SigmaStore's reach through a clean and minimalistic online catalogue that showcases our products without the complexity of a traditional e-commerce platform. We strive to provide a seamless browsing experience where customers can easily discover affordable fashion through intuitive navigation, thoughtfully organized categories, and visually engaging content.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become one of Malaysia's most trusted destinations for affordable vintage fashion. We aim to deliver a modern, responsive, and accessible website that reflects SigmaStore's identity through clean design, smooth interactions, and an enjoyable browsing experience across all devices.
