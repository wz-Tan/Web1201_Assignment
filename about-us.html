About SigmaStore


Our Story

Founded in Malaysia in 2026, SigmaStore was created with a simple goal: to make sustainable and affordable shopping accessible to everyone. We noticed that many communities lacked convenient access to thrift stores and budget-friendly alternatives, while consumerism continued to contribute to unnecessary waste and environmental impact. At SigmaStore, we believe that quality products should not come at the expense of either your wallet or the planet. By giving pre-loved and reusable items a second life, we help customers save money while reducing waste. Our platform is built on the principles of the three R's: Reduce, Reuse, and Recycle. This encourages smarter consumption habits and promoting a more sustainable future for generations to come.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide affordable, quality products through sustainable shopping practices that benefit both people and the environment. We strive to reduce waste, extend the lifecycle of usable goods, and make eco-conscious choices more accessible to everyday consumers. Through every item sold, we aim to foster a culture of responsible consumption, empower communities to embrace sustainability, and contribute to a greener future—one purchase at a time.

Our vision

To become Malaysia's trusted destination for sustainable shopping, where affordability, quality, and environmental responsibility come together to create lasting positive impact for our communities and the planet.
